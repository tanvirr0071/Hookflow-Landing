THE TABLE
A miniature grocery checkout on a dark walnut tabletop at night. One short conveyor counter with a small till and a card reader. A shelf wall of tiny blank packages behind it. At the far end of the table, a small bank building block with lit windows. A brass coin chute, like a marble run, links the cash side of the counter to the bank and on to a glass jar on the card side. Warm key light from upper left, cool teal fill from the right, practical glow from the bulb sign over the counter.
- Accent colour: points gold #F2B01E, used only for the coins, the POINTS jar and the hook sign.
- Hero prop: The brass coin chute and the glass POINTS jar it feeds, built as one component with states: empty, one coin rolling, flowing, jar full and glowing.
- Figurines: CASH, a matte green figurine, always on the left of the counter. CARD, a matte blue figurine, always on the right, with a blank card standing beside it. In shots 13 and 14 a small grey figurine and a larger gold one stand on the scale pans.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A the counter, low and close. B the counter from directly above. C macro on the till and card reader. D the bank end of the table. E the shelf wall. F a brass balance scale at the front edge of the table.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Anton" for signs and stamped numbers, "IBM Plex Mono" for receipts and source tags, "Oswald" for labels and the report cover.
