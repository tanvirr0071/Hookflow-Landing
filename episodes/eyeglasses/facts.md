# One company behind your glasses (EssilorLuxottica): facts

Series: Markup. Research date: 2026-10-23. Newest full year: **FY2025** (results released Feb 11, 2026). Newest quarter: **Q3 2026** (released Oct 20, 2026). FY2026 results are not out yet.

Brand check against the brief:
- **Ray-Ban, Oakley, Sunglass Hut, LensCrafters:** confirmed in the company's own Sept 2026 release.
- **Pearle Vision, EyeMed:** confirmed in Luxottica's SEC 20-F (they came with Cole National in 2004).
- **Target Optical:** the brief was right to be suspicious. It is run under a **licence from Target**, not owned. See fact 4.

## Myths

1. **MYTH: "Luxottica owns Target Optical."**
   - True: it operates Target Optical under a multi-year licence from Target Corporation, renewed effective Feb 12, 2023 (fact 4).
   - Say "runs," not "owns."

2. **MYTH: "Luxottica owns Prada, Chanel and every designer brand on the wall."**
   - True: luxury names are mostly licences. The company itself calls them "luxury licensed brands."
   - Prada's licence runs to 2030, with an option to 2035.
   - The brands it calls its own are Ray-Ban, Oakley and Supreme (fact 3, fact 13).

3. **MYTH: "Glasses cost $X to make, so the markup is exactly Y%."**
   - True: EssilorLuxottica does not disclose per-frame cost or markup. CBS said Luxottica "wouldn't tell us their markup."
   - The only usable line is 60 Minutes' "up to 20 times what they cost to make" (2012), attributed to CBS.

4. **CAREFUL: "It's an Italian company."**
   - True: Luxottica was Italian. EssilorLuxottica was formed in 2018 with France's Essilor; its releases are datelined Charenton-le-Pont, France, and its shares trade on Euronext Paris.
   - Say "the French-Italian giant."

5. **MYTH: "It's a legal monopoly / it owns every glasses brand (Warby Parker included)."**
   - True: big rivals exist. Warby Parker's FY2025 10-K names EssilorLuxottica and VSP as competitors.
   - Luxottica USA's CEO wrote in 2012 that "only a fraction of the 150 million frames and sunglasses sold every year" were made by Luxottica. Source: https://www.2020mag.com/article/luxottica-responds-to-60-minutes-segment-with-letter-to-customers-37298
   - Do not say "monopoly." Say "one company owns a huge share of the brands, the stores and even the insurance."

## Strongest hook number

**€28.5 billion** in FY2025 revenue from one company. It makes the Ray-Bans, the Oakleys and the lenses, and runs Sunglass Hut, LensCrafters and the counter inside Target (fact 1, fact 3).

Backup hook: "At least half a billion are wearing our glasses now," from the CEO on 60 Minutes in 2012 (fact 10).

## Strongest twist

Oakley was Luxottica's rival. After Luxottica bought Sunglass Hut, it cut Oakley's sunglasses there and Oakley sued. Six years later Luxottica simply bought Oakley for $2.1 billion. Its CEO's explanation: "they understood that life was better together" (facts 6, 7).

## Could change soon

- The share price is volatile: it jumped after Q3 2026, but Reuters says it had lost more than half its value in recent months.
- The Del Vecchio heir vs CEO Milleri fight over control of Delfin (32.4%) is unresolved.
- Meta's reported ~3% stake may grow toward 5%.
- FY2026 results (due around Feb 2027) will replace the FY2025 numbers.
