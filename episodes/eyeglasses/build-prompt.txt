THE TABLE
An optician's world on a pale grey tabletop. In the centre, a small square display ring like a boxing ring, with two display stands facing each other under a hanging bulb scoreboard. Along the back, a mall strip of five small store blocks with unlit lightbox signs. To one side, a judge's bench block with a gavel on a stand, and a contract desk with a lamp. Cool daylight key from upper right, soft warm fill, the scoreboard and store signs as practicals.
- Accent colour: lens amber #FFB03A, used only for the Oakley pair's lenses, the owner tags and the hook scoreboard. The set is cool grey-blue.
- Hero prop: A pair of sunglasses on a display stand with a brand tag on a string. Keep the frame a plain, generic rounded-rectangle shape. One component with states: tagged OAKLEY with amber lenses, lifted off its stand, re-tagged with an owner tag.
- Figurines: None are needed. If a shot feels empty, one grey figurine may stand at a store door.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A the display ring, low and close. B the mall strip, wide. C inside one store block: a display wall of pegs with blank frames. D the judge's bench. E the contract desk under its lamp. F directly above the whole table.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Anton" for the scoreboard, tags and stamps, "Oswald" for store signs and nameplates, "IBM Plex Mono" for small print and deeds.
