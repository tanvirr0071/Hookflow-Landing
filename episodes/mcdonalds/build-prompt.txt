THE TABLE
A property developer's model on a green felt tabletop. The felt is marked out in small land plots. On the plots stand restaurant building blocks: plain cream boxes with a dark roof, a lit window and a plain lettered sign. At the front edge sits a heavy brass rent box with a coin slot and a small LED counter. In one corner, a 1950s desk: a green-shaded lamp, an adding machine, a ledger. Dusk light: a warm key from upper right, cool blue fill, lit windows and the desk lamp as practicals.
- Accent colour: mint green #3FBF8F, used only for the rent box, its counter and the hook sign. Keep the restaurant blocks cream with dark teal roofs.
- Hero prop: One restaurant block that lifts off its plot, built as one component with states: lit and trading, lifted to show the plot plaque under it, stamped. It is the largest, best-lit block on the table.
- Figurines: FRANCHISEE, a matte orange figurine at the hero block's door; more orange figurines in the wide shots (a static row is fine). SONNEBORN, a charcoal figurine with a brimmed-hat shape, only at the desk. Three grey banker figurines in shot 06.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A low and close on the hero block and the rent box. B macro on the rent box counter. C the 1950s desk corner. D directly above the whole felt map. E macro on the hero block's plot and deed.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Anton" for signs, counters and stamps, "Oswald" for plaques and labels, "Special Elite" for the 1950s desk papers, "IBM Plex Mono" for small print.
