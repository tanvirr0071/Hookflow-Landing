## ElevenLabs script

```text
At AMC, snacks earn nearly as much as movies. AMC's average U.S. ticket: twelve seventy-eight. Plus eight fifty-seven on snacks and drinks.

About forty-eight cents per ticket dollar goes to studios.

Now the snack counter. The food itself costs about twenty cents per dollar.

So why did theaters ever let popcorn in?

Early movie palaces wanted nothing to do with it. They had rugs, and didn't want popcorn ground in.

Then the Depression hit. A bag of popcorn cost five or ten cents. The theaters that sold snacks survived.

Fast-forward to twenty twenty-five. Tickets kept one point three eight billion after studios. Snacks kept one point three four billion after food costs. Both before wages and rent.

Economists argue pricey popcorn keeps tickets cheaper. The snack they once refused now pays like movies.
```

Pronunciation: AMC = A-M-C (letters).
