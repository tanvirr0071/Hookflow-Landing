## ElevenLabs script

```text
Costco's dollar-fifty hot dog doesn't need profit. Over two hundred forty-five million combos in one year. Same price since the mid-eighties.

Craig Jelinek once warned co-founder Jim Sinegal. "Jim, we can't sell this hot dog for a buck fifty."

Sinegal answered. "If you raise the effing hot dog, I will kill you. Figure it out."

So Costco built two hot dog plants.

Why guard a hot dog this hard?

Last year, membership fees brought in about five point nine billion dollars. About half of Costco's operating profit. Combos bring in roughly a few hundred million dollars. A rounding error next to five point nine billion.

In twenty twenty-four, the card went up. The hot dog stayed a dollar fifty. Over nine in ten U.S. and Canadian members renew.

The hot dog's the ad that keeps them renewing. The money's in the sixty-five-dollar card.
```

Pronunciation: Sinegal = SIN-eh-gull · Jelinek = JEL-in-eck · Costco = COST-co.
