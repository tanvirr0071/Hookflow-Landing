## Myths

1. **MYTH: "Amex's $8.2B is pure profit" / "Delta makes more money from credit cards than from flying."**
   - True: The $8.2B is revenue, not profit. Amex buys miles that Delta later has to honor with real seats; "loyalty travel awards" were $4.2B of 2025 passenger revenue. Total passenger revenue ($51.8B) dwarfs the card money.
   - Safe framing: the card money is high-margin and bigger than Delta's $5.0B net profit.

2. **MYTH: "Delta sold SkyMiles in 2020 to survive."**
   - True: Delta borrowed $9B (notes due 2025 and 2028, plus a term loan) secured by SkyMiles assets. It kept the program.
   - In October 2026 Bastian said he is "reticent" to bring in a financial investor.

3. **MYTH: "Amex pays Delta $10 billion a year."**
   - True: $10B is the long-term target ("on the way to $10 billion"). Actuals are $8.2B in 2025 and more than $9B expected in 2026.

4. **MYTH: "The Delta card is 1% of U.S. GDP."**
   - True: Delta's own wording is "approaching" 1%. It is a company claim, not a measured figure.

5. **MYTH: "Delta's card is about 10% of Amex's business."**
   - True: Amex's own 10-K says about 13% of worldwide billed business and about 21% of worldwide card loans.

## Strongest hook number

**$8.2 billion.** American Express paid Delta $8.2B in 2025, more than Delta's entire $5.0B net profit for the year.

## Strongest twist

When the airline nearly died, the credit-card side saved it, twice.
- 2008: Amex prepaid $1B for miles.
- 2020: with planes grounded and $12.4B in losses, Delta borrowed $9B using its frequent-flyer program as collateral, "the largest debt financing in aviation history." Amex still paid $2.9B that year.

Today the CEO says he doesn't "dismiss the notion" that SkyMiles alone could be worth $75B or more.

## Could change soon

- Delta's FY2026 results (due around mid-January 2027) will replace the "more than $9B" guidance with an actual figure.
- The Amex agreement runs through the end of 2029 per Amex's 10-K. Some third-party blogs claim an early renewal in 2026, which I could not verify. Watch for an official renewal announcement.
- Delta's share price and market cap move daily. Do not compare the $75B SkyMiles figure to the market cap without a same-day quote.
