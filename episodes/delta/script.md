## ElevenLabs script

```text
Amex paid Delta more than Delta's entire profit. Eight point two billion dollars, in twenty twenty-five. Delta's net profit that year was five billion.

Amex buys miles that Delta must honor with seats. Look at everything charged on Amex cards worldwide. About thirteen percent goes on Delta cards. Amex's payment has more than doubled since twenty eighteen.

But what happens to that money in a crisis?

First, the two thousand eight crash. Amex prepaid Delta one billion dollars for miles it would use later.

Then came twenty twenty, and the planes sat parked. Delta lost twelve point four billion. It borrowed nine billion against its SkyMiles program. "The largest debt financing in aviation history," Delta said.

Yet Amex still paid two point nine billion.

That year, Delta's profit vanished. The Amex money didn't.
```

Pronunciation: Amex = AM-ex · SkyMiles = SKY-miles (one word).
