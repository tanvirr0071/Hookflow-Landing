THE TABLE
A miniature gym on a pale tabletop, built like a doll's house with the front wall and roof removed: three rows of tiny treadmills (simple wedge shapes with a moving belt), a front desk with a turnstile, an LED counter over the door. Outside the door, the bare tabletop where the crowd stands. Bright, clean light inside from a soft overhead key; the table outside is dimmer and cooler, so the gym glows.
- Accent colour: coral #FF6B5A, used only for the member counter, the coins and the hook signs. Walls are deep teal; the floor is pale grey.
- Hero prop: The front door with its LED member counter above and a brass capacity plaque beside it. One component with states: counter rolling, counter locked, door jammed, door quiet.
- Figurines: This episode needs a crowd, so the three-figurine limit does not apply to it: the crowd is a static block of several hundred small grey figurines outside the door, moved only as a group (one slide, one shuffle). Inside the gym there are never more than three, in coral. One teal figurine behind the front desk is the manager.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A low and wide on the door from outside, the crowd in the foreground. B directly above the gym and the crowd. C the front desk, close. D inside, along a row of treadmills. E macro on the desk top (papers, cards, receipt printer). F a wall calendar on the gym's back wall.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Anton" for the counter, signs and stamps, "Oswald" for plaques, posters and the quote board, "IBM Plex Mono" for receipts and small print.
