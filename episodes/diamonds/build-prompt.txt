VOICE-OVER (one line per shot; the audio is the source of truth)
01 "A diamond is forever" is an ad slogan.
02 A copywriter wrote it in nineteen forty-seven.
03 For De Beers, the diamond giant.
04 Before World War Two,
05 about one engagement ring in ten had a diamond.
06 Decades of ads later, about eight in ten.
07 Then came diamonds grown in a lab.
08 In twenty eighteen, De Beers sold them too.
09 Eight hundred dollars a carat.
10 Its own CEO said they "may not be forever."
11 Seven years later, prices had fallen ninety percent.
12 A later CEO said supermarkets sell them for two hundred.
13 So De Beers moved to shut its lab brand.
14 For couples married last year,
15 sixty-one percent of rings were lab-grown.
16 Now De Beers itself is for sale.
17 The slogan lasted. The price didn't.

THE TABLE
A jeweller's bench as a tabletop at night. A black velvet pad in the centre with an open ring box. Behind it, a 1940s magazine propped on a small brass easel. To the left, a typewriter. To the right, a glass dome on a brass base (the lab), then a tiny supermarket shelf of blank cereal boxes. A ten-slot velvet ring tray at the front edge. One hard, small key light from upper left so the stone throws sparkles; a dim warm fill; the lab dome and a lightbox strip as practicals.
- Accent colour: ice blue #7FD6FF, used only for diamond glints, the lab glow and the hook sign. Everything else is black velvet, brass and cream paper.
- Hero prop: A solitaire ring in an open ring box with a price tag on a string. The stone is real faceted geometry with a glassy, refractive material and animated sparkle. One component with states: glowing with tag, tag showing a price, tag cut and falling, tag gone.
- Figurines: A cream figurine at the typewriter (the copywriter). Two small figurines side by side on a white round plinth (the couple) in shot 14. No others.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A macro on the ring box with the magazine behind it. B the typewriter. C the ten-slot ring tray from directly above. D the lab dome. E the supermarket shelf. F a small mine-office block with a signpost at the back of the table.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Playfair Display" for the magazine ad and the gift box, "Special Elite" for the typewriter page and folder, "Anton" for tags, flap boards and stamps, "IBM Plex Mono" for small print.

THE FIRST SECOND

A lightbox strip along the easel's foot reads "“FOREVER” WAS AN AD" and is on its second flicker, fully readable. Nothing fades in.

SHOTS (one per voice-over line; the shot cuts in on its CUT word; quoted strings are in-world copy)
01 THE RING AND THE AD — CUT "A". Setup A, built as <HookFrame /> (see THE FIRST SECOND). The magazine page reads "“A DIAMOND IS FOREVER”"; the lightbox strip reads "“FOREVER” WAS AN AD". On "forever" the stone throws a sparkle across the lens. On "ad" a rubber stamp lands on the corner of the magazine page and leaves "AD" in red (impact frame).
02 THE TYPEWRITER — CUT "A". Setup B, macro. Keys strike by themselves and the page climbs out of the roller. On "nineteen" the typed date clears the roller: "1947". A brass nameplate on the desk reads "FRANCES GERETY · N.W. AYER". The cream figurine stands beside the machine.
03 THE CLIENT — CUT "For". Setup B. The page is pulled out and drops into an open folder. On "De Beers" the folder snaps shut and its stamped cover fills the frame: "CLIENT: DE BEERS".
04 THE TRAY — CUT "Before". Setup C, top-down on the ten-slot tray, every slot holding a plain gold band. On "War" a calendar card slides in beside it: "BEFORE WWII".
05 ONE IN TEN — CUT "about". Setup C, slow push in. On "one" a single stone clicks onto one band and lights ice blue. On "ten" a brass label at the tray's edge flips to "1 IN 10".
06 EIGHT IN TEN — CUT "Decades". Setup C. On "ads" magazine pages flutter across the lens (foreground wipe). Behind them stones click onto seven more bands, one after another. On "eight" the label flips to "8 IN 10"; small print: "LATE 20TH CENTURY".
07 THE LAB — CUT "Then". Setup D. Low and close on the glass dome. On "grown" a seed inside glows ice blue and a stone grows on it, facet by facet. On "lab" the brass plaque on the base catches the light: "LAB-GROWN"; small print: "FTC, 2018: “THUS, THEY ARE DIAMONDS.”".
08 THE GIFT BOX — CUT "In". Setup D to A in one move. The grown stone is set into a small gift box. On "De Beers" its lid slides on, lettered "LIGHTBOX · BY DE BEERS · 2018".
09 THE PRICE — CUT "Eight". Setup A, macro on the hero ring's tag. On "Eight" it spins to face the lens and stops: "$800 / CARAT".
10 THE CARD — CUT "Its". Setup A. A small printed card stands in the gift box lid. On "may" rack focus onto it: "“MAY NOT BE FOREVER”", with the credit "— BRUCE CLEAVER, DE BEERS CEO, 2018".
11 THE FALL — CUT "Seven". Setup A, tilt down. On "fallen" the tag's string is snipped and the tag drops down a tall brass ruler. On "ninety" it hits the bottom and a flap board there flips to "−90%" (impact frame); small print: "WHOLESALE, SINCE 2018 · DE BEERS, MAY 2025".
12 THE SUPERMARKET — CUT "A". Setup E. Eye level at the tiny shelf: ring boxes between blank cereal boxes. On "supermarkets" the shelf light buzzes on. On "two hundred" the shelf strip under the rings flips to "$200"; small print: "“IN SUPERMARKETS FOR $200” — AL COOK, DE BEERS CEO, 2025".
13 CLOSING — CUT "So". Setup A. The gift box. On "shut" its lid drops closed and a stamp lands across it: "CLOSING" (impact frame); small print: "ANNOUNCED MAY 2025".
14 THE COUPLE — CUT "For". Setup C, low beside the ring tray. The two figurines stand on their white plinth. On "married" a calendar card flips to "MARRIED IN 2025".
15 SIX IN TEN — CUT "sixty-one". Setup C, top-down on the tray. On "sixty-one" six of the ten stones switch to the ice-blue lab glow, one after another, and an LED strip on the tray edge locks on "61% LAB-GROWN"; small print: "THE KNOT 2026 STUDY · U.S. ENGAGEMENT RINGS".
16 FOR SALE — CUT "Now". Setup F. A small mine-office block lettered "DE BEERS". On "sale" a signpost is hammered into the table in front of it: "FOR SALE" (small shake); small print on the post: "REPORTED PRICE ≈ $1 BILLION · BLOOMBERG".
17 BACK TO THE RING — CUT "The". Setup A. The magazine page still reads "“A DIAMOND IS FOREVER”". On "price" the hero ring's last scrap of string falls away into the dark. Then the return move described in THE LAST SECOND, the strip reading "“FOREVER” WAS AN AD".
