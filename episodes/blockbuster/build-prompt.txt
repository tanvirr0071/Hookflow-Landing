THE TABLE
A boardroom and a retail empire on one dark slate tabletop. At the front, a long model boardroom table with small chairs under a hanging lamp. Behind it, a glass office tower block. Across the rest of the slate, rows of small video-store blocks: plain plum-coloured boxes with a white lightbox sign. Cool evening light: a soft key from upper left, a warm practical from the boardroom lamp, lit store signs as bokeh.
- Accent colour: tag orange #FF6A2B, used only for the $50 MILLION price tag and the hook sign.
- Hero prop: A kraft envelope lettered NETFLIX with an orange price tag on a string, built as one component with states: offered (tag swinging), stamped NO, weighed, dwarfed.
- Figurines: Two tan figurines, the Netflix founders. One taller charcoal figurine, the Blockbuster CEO, at the head of the table. One grey figurine beside it, the lawyer.
- Setups (reuse them; do not add more): A the boardroom table, low and close. B macro on the envelope and tag. C the tower block from outside. D the boardroom from directly above. E a ledger and a brass balance scale at the table's end. F the field of store blocks, wide and from above. G low and close on one store block.
- Fonts (local files, only the weights used): "Anton" for signs, tags and stamps, "Oswald" for nameplates and boards, "Special Elite" for the book page and the notepad header, "Permanent Marker" for the handwritten price, "IBM Plex Mono" for small print.

THE FIRST SECOND
Setup A, camera low, already pushing in. The envelope is mid-slide across the boardroom table toward the charcoal figurine, its orange tag swinging. A rubber stamp hangs a few centimetres above the table, already falling. The lightbox on the wall behind reads "BLOCKBUSTER SAID NO TO NETFLIX", fully lit. Nothing fades in.

SHOTS (one per voice-over line; the shot cuts in on its CUT word; quoted strings are in-world copy)
01 THE OFFER — CUT "Blockbuster". Setup A, built as <HookFrame /> (see THE FIRST SECOND). The wall lightbox reads "BLOCKBUSTER SAID NO TO NETFLIX"; the envelope is lettered "NETFLIX". On "no" the stamp lands beside the envelope and leaves "NO" in red (impact frame).
02 THE TAG — CUT "The". Setup B, macro. The orange tag is still swinging from the stamp's hit. On "fifty" it spins to face the lens and stops: "$50 MILLION".
03 THE TOWER — CUT "September". Setup C. Low wide on the glass tower block at dusk. On "September" a desk calendar in the foreground flips to "SEPT 2000". On "Dallas" a small street sign at the tower's foot swings into the light: "DALLAS".
04 THE FLIGHT — CUT "Netflix's". Setup C. On "flew" a tiny model plane on a wire arcs across the frame, close to the lens. On "pitch" the two tan figurines hop to the tower door with the envelope between them. Out: the plane wipes the lens.
05 THE QUESTION — CUT "Blockbuster's". Setup D, top-down on the boardroom table: tan figurines on one side, charcoal and grey on the other. On "asked" the grey figurine pushes a notepad to the centre, its header reading "YOUR PRICE?".
06 THE NUMBER — CUT "Reed". Setup D, punch in on the notepad. On "Reed" one tan figurine hops forward. A marker writes "“FIFTY MILLION.”" under the header, stroke by stroke, finishing on "million". Small print at the foot of the pad: "— REED HASTINGS, AS TOLD BY MARC RANDOLPH".
07 THE CEO — CUT "A". Setup A, low angle on the charcoal figurine at the head of the table. On "CEO" the brass nameplate in front of it catches the lamp: "BLOCKBUSTER · CEO". The figurine begins to tremble very slightly.
08 THE LAUGH — CUT "was". Setup A. On "struggling" a book page swings into the foreground, sharp, with one underlined line: "“STRUGGLING NOT TO LAUGH”" and the credit "— MARC RANDOLPH, “THAT WILL NEVER WORK”". On "laugh" the charcoal figurine rocks back on its base and the chairs rattle.
09 THE STAMP AGAIN — CUT "But". Setup B, macro on the red "NO" stamp mark. On "sense" rack focus past it to a ledger at the end of the table, its spine reading "NETFLIX · 2000".
10 THE LOSS — CUT "That". Setup E. The ledger falls open. On "fifty-seven" a line stamps itself across the page in red: "LOSS: $57.4 MILLION" (impact frame); small print: "NETFLIX S-1 FILING".
11 THE SCALE — CUT "More". Setup E, the brass scale. The orange "$50 MILLION" tag sits on the left pan. On "More" a red iron weight cast with "$57.4M" is set on the right pan and that side sinks to the table.
12 THE EMPIRE — CUT "At". Setup F, wide from above. On "peak" the store blocks' signs switch on in a wave that crosses the whole table. On "nine thousand" a flap board at the table's edge flips to "≈ 9,000 STORES"; small print: "PEAK · END OF 2004".
13 THE FLICKER — CUT "So". Setup G, low and close on one store block. Its sign flickers. On "killed" a tag on a string swings in front of it: "WHAT KILLED IT?".
14 THE ANSWER — CUT "Reed". Setup G, pan to a tan figurine standing by a small bulb-lit quote board on the slate. On "answer" its bulbs warm up and the header reads "REED HASTINGS · 2009".
15 THE DEBT — CUT "If". Setup G. The board's first line lights: "“IF IT HADN’T BEEN FOR THEIR DEBT,". On "debt" an iron weight on a chain is lowered into frame above the store block, cast with "NEARLY $1 BILLION IN DEBT".
16 THE QUOTE — CUT "they". Setup G. The board's second line lights: "THEY COULD HAVE KILLED US.”", with the credit "— REED HASTINGS, 2009, VIA CNBC" under it. The weight sways just above the store's roof.
17 BANKRUPT — CUT "Blockbuster". Setup G. On "bankrupt" the weight drops onto the store block, its sign buzzes out and dust jumps off the slate (impact frame). On "twenty" a stamp lands on the slate in front of it: "CHAPTER 11 · SEPT 23, 2010".
18 TEN HOURS — CUT "Netflix". Setup B. The orange "$50 MILLION" tag again, now hanging from a desk clock whose hand sweeps a full turn in the shot. On "ten" an LED strip under the clock locks on "EVERY ≈ 10 HOURS"; small print: "2025 REVENUE: $45.2 BILLION".
19 THE LAST STORE — CUT "One". Setup F, wide. Every store sign is dark except one small block at the table's edge. On "Bend" a road sign beside it catches the light: "BEND, OREGON". Then the return move described in THE LAST SECOND, ending on the lightbox "BLOCKBUSTER SAID NO TO NETFLIX".
